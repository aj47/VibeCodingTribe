# Vibe Coding Tribe — 30s vertical promo

## Viewer promise

Before you ship a vibe-coded product into the void, get real builders to test it, catch what AI misses, and turn feedback into momentum.

## Beat map

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.5s | Bug-catching hero art, fast push-in | YOU BUILT IT. / NOW BREAK IT. | You built it. Now break it.
| 4.5–9.5s | Mission-routing art | STOP SHIPPING / INTO THE VOID. | Before you ship your vibe-coded app, get real people to test it.
| 9.5–14.5s | Verified-feedback art | POST A MISSION. / GET REAL FEEDBACK. | Vibe Coding Tribe connects builders with testers who catch the bugs AI misses.
| 14.5–19.5s | Human + agent room art | BUILD → TEST → / SHIP BETTER. | Post a mission. Give feedback.
| 19.5–24.5s | Credits / reputation art | EARN CREDITS. / BUILD REPUTATION. | Earn credits and reputation.
| 24.5–30.0s | Brand lockup / CTA | JOIN THE TRIBE | Build faster, with proof. Join the tribe at vibecodingtribe.com.

## Production notes

- Canvas: 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4.
- Style: dark editorial cards with high-contrast Manrope/Arial type, product art as proof, blue CTA accents.
- Motion: hard-cut scene changes, 6% card push-ins, quick text fade/slide entrances, per-scene pulse hits.
- Text is intentionally sparse and phone-readable; the narration carries the connective tissue.
