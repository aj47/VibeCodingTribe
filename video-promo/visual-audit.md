## What is working

- The first frame states a sharp tension immediately: “YOU BUILT IT. / NOW BREAK IT.”
- The product-art cards remain the visual proof and sit inside one consistent optical-center lane.
- The copy is sparse enough to read on a phone, with one dominant idea per beat.
- The final CTA uses the existing brand lockup and the real project domain.
- Render verification confirms a 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30-second H.264/AAC file.

## Minor notes

- The motion is deliberately restrained: subtle push-ins plus hard cuts. A future v2 could add one motivated punch-in or sound accent on the hook-to-mission transition.
- The promo is sound-on by design, but the on-screen copy is complete enough to work muted.
